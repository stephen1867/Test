Absolutely. Our co-managed model gives your internal team enterprise-grade tooling, a 24/7 safety net, and deep specialists on demand — so they can focus on projects instead of password resets.

A dedicated transition team documents your environment, deploys our management stack, and coordinates with your previous provider. Typical timeline is 10 business days with zero user downtime. You'll barely notice — which is the point.

No. We earn your business monthly. Every plan is cancel-anytime with 30 days' notice, and we'll hand over complete documentation if you ever leave. Nobody's leaving, though.
